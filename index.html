Using your collar

Charge it. Fit it.
Head outside.

1

Charge before use

Connect the collar to a suitable USB power source. Do not use it while it is connected to power.

2

Adjust the fit

Set the strap to your dog’s measured neck range. Confirm the collar feels secure and comfortably fitted before each walk.

3

Switch it on

Turn the collar on before leaving and confirm the illuminated section is working. Exact controls may vary with the supplied model.

4

Check after the walk

Switch it off, inspect it for damage, and recharge when needed. Keep it dry unless a verified water-resistance rating is provided.

The illuminated collar adds visibility; it does not replace a leash, supervision, reflective gear, or safe walking practices.
