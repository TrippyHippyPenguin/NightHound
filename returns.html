Issue window: 7 days from confirmed delivery

Policy overview

Because NightHound products are prepared and fulfilled for each order, all sales are final except where an item arrives damaged, defective, nonfunctional, incorrect, or materially different from the item ordered.

Eligible issues

  • The item arrives damaged.
  • The LED lighting does not function.
  • The charging function does not work.
  • The wrong product was sent.
  • The wrong size was sent compared with the size ordered.
  • The wrong collar color was sent compared with the color ordered.
  • The product is materially different from what was ordered.

How to report an issue

Contact NightHound within 7 days of confirmed delivery. Include your order number, an explanation of the issue, and clear photographs and/or video showing the problem when applicable.

After review, NightHound may provide a replacement or refund where appropriate. If NightHound made a fulfillment error or the product arrived damaged or defective, the customer should not be charged simply to correct NightHound’s mistake.

A direct support email has not yet been published. It will be added before online ordering opens.

Generally not eligible

  • Change of mind.
  • Ordering the wrong size when the selected size was correctly fulfilled.
  • Deciding a selected color is no longer wanted.
  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Chewing damage, misuse, improper charging, intentional damage, modification, or improper care.

Customers are responsible for reviewing the published sizing chart before purchasing.
